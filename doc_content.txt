Abstract. 
The Free Nutritious Meal Program (MBG) is one of Indonesia’s strategic initiatives to improve the nutritional status of students. However, its implementation still faces several challenges, including limited monitoring of food distribution, manual nutritional evaluation, and ineffective management of food waste, which may reach 40–50 kg per school each day. This study proposes 
SmartMBG
, a web-based monitoring platform integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI), 
WebGIS
, and circular economy principles to support the implementation of the MBG program. The proposed platform enables multi-stakeholder collaboration among schools, Nutrition Fulfillment Service Units (SPPG), government administrators, and organic waste management partners through integrated monitoring, AI-based nutritional analysis, interactive geographic visualization, food waste reporting, and service evaluation. The research employs requirement analysis, system design, web application development, AI and 
WebGIS
 integration, and functional testing. The expected outputs include a functional 
SmartMBG
 platform, technical documentation, scientific publication, and intellectual property registration. The proposed system is expected to improve monitoring efficiency, support data-driven decision-making, and encourage sustainable food waste management through a circular economy approach. Furthermore, this research contributes to the achievement of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) 2, 3, 9, 12, and 17.

1.
  
 
Introduction
The Free Nutritious Meal Program (MBG) is one of Indonesia's national strategic programs aimed at improving students' nutritional quality while supporting the development of healthy and productive human resources. Through this program, nutritious meals are distributed daily to students across Indonesia. Besides improving nutritional intake, the program is expected to reduce stunting, improve students' learning concentration, and strengthen national food 
security. However, the implementation of MBG still faces several challenges related to monitoring, nutritional evaluation, and food waste management. Therefore, this research proposes 
SmartMBG
, an integrated web-based monitoring platform that combines Artificial Intelligence (AI), 
WebGIS
, and circular economy principles to support the implementation of the MBG program. 
1.1 Research Background 
The implementation of the Free Nutritious Meal Program involves various stakeholders, including schools, Nutrition Fulfillment Service Units (SPPG), government agencies, and organic waste management partners. Although the program has significant social benefits, several operational challenges remain unresolved.
One of the major issues is the lack of an integrated monitoring system. Currently, food distribution reports, service evaluations, and monitoring activities are generally conducted manually or through separate information systems, making data collection inefficient and limiting real-time decision-making.
Another important issue concerns food waste generated from the MBG program. According to 
Komunitas
 
Nol
 
Sampah
 Surabaya, approximately 
40–50 kg of food waste
 can be generated each day from one school serving around 3,000 students. Most of this organic waste is discarded without proper management, despite its potential economic value as compost or maggot feed within a circular economy framework.
At the same time, technological developments in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and 
WebGIS
 offer opportunities to improve monitoring effectiveness. AI can automate food recognition, nutritional analysis, and food waste estimation, while 
WebGIS
 enables interactive visualization of schools, SPPG, and waste management partners to support data-driven decision-making.
Therefore, an integrated digital platform is required to improve monitoring efficiency while promoting sustainable food waste management.
1.2 Related Works 
Several previous studies have investigated digital systems for food monitoring, nutritional analysis, and geographic information systems. AI-based approaches have demonstrated promising performance in recognizing food images and estimating nutritional content using computer vision techniques. Meanwhile, 
WebGIS
 has been widely applied for spatial monitoring, public service mapping, and resource distribution.
Other studies have explored circular economy approaches by utilizing organic waste as raw material for compost and maggot cultivation. However, these studies generally focus on a single domain and do not integrate nutritional monitoring, geographic information systems, and food waste management into one comprehensive platform.
Consequently, stakeholders still need multiple independent systems to monitor MBG implementation, reducing operational efficiency and limiting information sharing.

1.3 Research Gap and Novelty 
Although previous studies have demonstrated the potential of Artificial Intelligence, 
WebGIS
, and food waste management independently, very few studies have integrated these technologies into a single platform supporting the implementation of the Free Nutritious Meal Program.
The novelty of 
SmartMBG
 lies in the integration of:
Artificial Intelligence for food recognition, nutritional analysis, and food waste estimation.
WebGIS
 for interactive mapping of schools, SPPG, and waste management partners.
Multi-actor monitoring involving government administrators, schools, SPPG, and organic waste partners.
Circular economy implementation through digital monitoring of organic waste utilization.
A web-based decision-support platform for monitoring the MBG program.
These innovations distinguish 
SmartMBG
 from previous research and provide a more comprehensive solution for monitoring the implementation of the MBG program.
1.4 Research Objectives and Contributions 
This research aims to develop 
SmartMBG
, an integrated web-based monitoring platform that supports the implementation of the Free Nutritious Meal Program using Artificial Intelligence and 
WebGIS
 technologies.
The expected contributions of this research include:
Developing an integrated monitoring platform connecting schools, SPPG, government administrators, and waste management partners.
Implementing Artificial Intelligence for automatic food recognition, nutritional analysis, and food waste estimation.
Integrating 
WebGIS
 to visualize the spatial distribution of schools, SPPG, and waste management partners.
Supporting circular economy implementation through digital monitoring of organic waste utilization.
Providing a decision-support system to improve the effectiveness, transparency, and sustainability of the MBG program.

2.
  
 
Method
This research employed the 
Research and Development (R&D)
 approach to design and develop 
SmartMBG
, an integrated web-based platform for monitoring the implementation of Indonesia's Free Nutritious Meal Program (MBG). The proposed system integrates Artificial Intelligence (AI), 
WebGIS
, and circular economy concepts to support nutritional monitoring, food waste management, and spatial information services.
The research consisted of several sequential stages, including problem identification, literature review, system requirements analysis, system design, system development, implementation of Artificial Intelligence and 
WebGIS
, system testing and evaluation, and documentation of research outputs. Figure 2 illustrates the overall research methodology adopted in this study.
The system was developed using 
Laravel
 as the backend framework, 
React.js
 as the frontend framework, 
PostgreSQL
 as the database management system, 
Leaflet.js
 for 
WebGIS
 visualization, and 
YOLO-based Computer Vision
 to perform food recognition and nutritional estimation. Functional verification of the developed system was conducted using 
Black Box Testing
, while system acceptance was evaluated based on the successful execution of each functional module.
2.1 Research Design 
The research adopted the 
Research and Development (R&D)
 methodology because the primary objective was to develop an integrated software platform rather than testing a specific hypothesis. The development process followed several iterative stages beginning with problem identification and ending with system evaluation.
Initially, the existing implementation of the MBG program was analyzed to identify operational problems associated with monitoring, nutritional evaluation, and food waste management. Subsequently, literature related to Artificial Intelligence, 
WebGIS
, food waste management, and circular economy was reviewed to establish the theoretical foundation for system development.
Based on the identified requirements, 
SmartMBG
 was designed using a modular architecture consisting of Artificial Intelligence, 
WebGIS
, and monitoring dashboard components. The system architecture was then implemented into a web-based application, followed by functional testing and system evaluation.

2.2 System Architecture 
The 
SmartMBG
 platform adopts a 
three-layer architecture
 consisting of the 
Presentation Layer
, 
Application Layer
, and 
Data Layer
. This layered architecture is designed to improve system scalability, maintainability, and communication among stakeholders while supporting the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and 
WebGIS
 technologies.
The 
Presentation Layer
 provides web-based user interfaces for four categories of users, namely 
government administrators, schools, 
Satuan
 
Pelayanan
 
Pemenuhan
 
Gizi
 (SPPG), and waste management partners
. Through this interface, users can perform activities such as monitoring food distribution, submitting food waste reports, accessing nutritional analysis results, managing spatial information, and generating monitoring reports according to their respective roles.
The 
Application Layer
 serves as the core of the 
SmartMBG
 platform by managing business logic using the 
Laravel
 framework. This layer also provides 
RESTful APIs
 to facilitate communication between the frontend application and backend services. Furthermore, it integrates 
Artificial Intelligence (AI)
 for food recognition, nutritional analysis, and food waste estimation, as well as 
WebGIS
 services
 using Leaflet to visualize the locations of schools, SPPG, and waste management partners interactively.
The 
Data Layer
 utilizes 
PostgreSQL
 as the centralized database management system to store and manage user information, food distribution records, nutritional analysis results, food waste reports, geographic coordinates, and AI prediction outputs. Centralized data storage ensures data consistency, supports efficient information retrieval, and enables seamless integration among all system modules.

 visualization, and food waste management within a single web platform.

3.2 Artificial Intelligence Implementation
The Artificial Intelligence module was implemented to support automatic food recognition and nutritional analysis. Users upload food images through the web application, after which the AI model detects food objects and estimates their nutritional composition.
The AI module also estimates food waste by analyzing images of leftover meals. The prediction results are stored in the centralized database and displayed through the monitoring dashboard to support decision-making.
The implementation of Artificial Intelligence improves monitoring efficiency by reducing manual nutritional assessment while providing consistent and objective estimation results.

3.5 Discussion
The implementation of 
SmartMBG
 demonstrates the feasibility of integrating Artificial Intelligence, 
WebGIS
, and circular economy concepts into a single monitoring platform. Unlike previous studies that mainly focused on individual components such as nutritional analysis or geographic information systems, 
SmartMBG
 combines multiple technologies to support end-to-end monitoring of the MBG program.
The integration of AI enables automatic food recognition and nutritional estimation, reducing manual analysis and improving consistency. Meanwhile, the 
WebGIS
 module enhances spatial monitoring by providing interactive visualization of schools, SPPG, and waste management partners.
In addition, the food waste reporting module supports circular economy implementation by facilitating collaboration between schools and waste management partners for organic waste utilization. This integrated approach contributes to improving operational efficiency, transparency, and decision-making within the MBG ecosystem.
Overall, the research demonstrates that 
SmartMBG
 provides a comprehensive digital solution capable of supporting sustainable implementation of the Free Nutritious Meal Program.
4.
  
 
Conclusion
This study successfully achieved its research objective by designing and developing 
SmartMBG
, an integrated web-based platform to support the implementation of Indonesia's 
Free Nutritious Meal Program (MBG)
. The proposed platform integrates 
Artificial Intelligence (AI)
, 
WebGIS
, and 
circular economy
 principles to facilitate nutritional analysis, food waste management, geographic visualization, and monitoring services within a unified information system. The developed architecture enables collaboration among government administrators, schools, Nutrition Fulfillment Service Units (SPPG), and waste management partners through centralized data management and real-time monitoring, thereby improving transparency, operational efficiency, and evidence-based decision-making in the implementation of the MBG program.
The primary contribution of this research is the development of an integrated digital platform that combines AI-based nutritional analysis, 
WebGIS
-based spatial monitoring, and circular economy concepts for sustainable food waste management. Unlike previous studies that generally focus on a single aspect of food monitoring or geographic information systems, 
SmartMBG
 provides a comprehensive solution by integrating multiple technologies and stakeholders into a single platform. Nevertheless, this research has several limitations. The Artificial Intelligence module was developed and evaluated using a limited dataset, while the system evaluation primarily focused on functional testing without extensive field implementation involving multiple schools and SPPG units. Therefore, future research 
should improve the AI model using larger and more diverse datasets, conduct broader pilot implementations in different regions, integrate Internet of Things (IoT) technologies for real-time monitoring, and develop a mobile application to improve accessibility and scalability. These future developments are expected to strengthen 
SmartMBG
 as a sustainable digital ecosystem that supports data-driven decision-making and contributes to the successful implementation of the Free Nutritious Meal Program in Indonesia.
